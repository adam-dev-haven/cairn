## Sources behind the training content

The exercise cues, band guidance, progressions and pain rules were written with reference to the following. Cairn is not affiliated with any of them, and none of them has reviewed this software.

- Training for Climbing, [tendon loading and recovery](https://trainingforclimbing.com/stronger-tendons-fingers-climbing/) and [climber's elbow rehabilitation](https://trainingforclimbing.com/treating-climbers-elbow-medial-epicondylitis/)
- Strength Climbing, [elbow pain causes and prevention](https://strengthclimbing.com/elbow-pain-the-ultimate-guide-for-rock-climbers/)
- Lopes et al. 2019, elastic versus conventional resistance training, [meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30815258/)
- Schoenfeld et al. 2017, low- versus high-load resistance training, [meta-analysis](https://journals.lww.com/nsca-jscr/fulltext/2017/12000/strength_and_hypertrophy_adaptations_between_low_.31.aspx)
- [Elastic band tension reference values](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4868225/)
- [Resistance band stacking and progressive overload](https://bodymotionlab.com/blog/resistance-band-stacking-progressive-overload-guide)
- [Calisthenics skills list](https://dieringe.com/blog/calisthenics-skills) and [skills with progressions](https://www.gymnasetips.com/calisthenics-skills/)
- [Door-anchor band exercise reference](https://thefitnessphantom.com/resistance-band-door-exercises-with-pdf)

## Typefaces

The app uses the system fonts (SF Pro) through SwiftUI. No font files are bundled.

## Not medical advice

Cairn logs training and applies simple rules to it. It does not diagnose or treat injuries. Its elbow and pain guidance is general, and anyone with persistent or sharp pain should see a clinician.
